# Product Rules

The canonical authority is [the Product Constitution](../docs/blueprint/00-product-constitution.md). Agents must read it before product-relevant work.

- Keep the patient central and optimize complete workflows, not feature count.
- Preserve speed, user momentum, durable truth, auditability, privacy, and migration provenance.
- Never infer clinical, controlled-substance, authentication, tenant-isolation, or financial policy from implementation convenience.
- Escalate undefined or conflicting product behavior to the founder.
