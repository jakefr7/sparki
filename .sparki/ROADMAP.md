# Roadmap

1. **Phase 1 — Trustworthy Engineering Baseline (`READY_FOR_FOUNDER_REVIEW`):** deterministic, isolated, and honest repository verification established; see [PHASE_1_CLOSEOUT.md](./PHASE_1_CLOSEOUT.md).
2. **Phase 2 — Authentication & Tenant Safety (`ACTIVE`):** address identity propagation, authorization defaults, and tenant-isolation guarantees.
3. **Phase 3 — Clinical Workflow Integrity:** verify lifecycle, discharge blocking, records, and clinical safety behavior.
4. **Phase 4 — Inventory Integrity Hardening:** strengthen type safety, persistence guarantees, counts, usage, controlled inventory, and auditability.
5. **Phase 5 — Honest External / Financial Workflows:** replace simulated delivery and placeholders with honest integration, payment, and ledger behavior.
