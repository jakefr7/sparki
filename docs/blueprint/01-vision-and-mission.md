# 01 Vision And Mission

## Constitutional Authority

This document inherits from the [Product Constitution](./00-product-constitution.md). If it conflicts with the Constitution, the Constitution governs.

## Purpose

Clarify what Sparki exists to build and why the work matters.

## Goals

- Define the long-term ambition for Sparki.
- Align product, engineering, design, sales, and support around one mission.
- Provide language that can guide hiring, fundraising, roadmap, and customer conversations.

## Guiding Questions

- What does the veterinary clinic of the future feel like to work in?
- What should Sparki make possible that legacy systems do not?
- Which parts of veterinary work should become invisible, automated, or dramatically faster?
- What must remain deeply human?

## Known Decisions

- Sparki is not trying to build another veterinary practice management system.
- Sparki is building an operating system for modern veterinary medicine.
- The product should improve the lived experience of the clinical workday.

## Open Questions

- What is the simplest durable mission statement?
- Which customer segment should shape the earliest version of the product?
- How should Sparki describe itself differently to clinics, investors, and candidates?

## Notes

Avoid brand theater. The strongest vision language should come from true product constraints and real clinic pain.
