# 02 Product Philosophy

## Constitutional Authority

This document inherits from the [Product Constitution](./00-product-constitution.md). If it conflicts with the Constitution, the Constitution governs.

## Purpose

Document how Sparki thinks about product quality, workflow design, AI, speed, migration, and operational usefulness.

## Goals

- Convert company beliefs into design and product standards.
- Help teams make consistent product choices without waiting for executive review.
- Define what "world-class" means for veterinary software.

## Guiding Questions

- Where do existing systems interrupt clinical flow?
- Which clicks are unavoidable, and which are artifacts of poor design?
- When should Sparki automate, suggest, summarize, or stay silent?
- How should product teams balance power, simplicity, and speed?

## Known Decisions

- Workflow quality matters more than feature count.
- Speed is a core product feature, not a performance optimization phase.
- AI should be ambient and useful rather than performative.
- Patient context should anchor clinical, communication, billing, and follow-up work.

## Open Questions

- What interaction patterns should be universal across Sparki?
- How should the team measure workflow friction?
- What product behaviors should be explicitly forbidden?
