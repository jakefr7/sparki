# Prompts

Reusable prompts for AI-assisted research, documentation, product thinking, and review.

## Constitutional Authority

All prompts inherit from the [Product Constitution](../blueprint/00-product-constitution.md). Prompts should reference the Constitution rather than restating its principles.

## Purpose

Help the team use AI consistently without allowing AI to invent product truth.

## Goals

- Standardize AI collaboration patterns.
- Encourage clear assumptions and open questions.
- Reduce repetitive documentation work.
- Preserve Sparki's product voice and principles.

## Guiding Questions

- What context does the AI need to avoid guessing?
- What should the AI explicitly not do?
- How should output separate facts, assumptions, decisions, and questions?
