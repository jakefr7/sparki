# 03 Industry Research

## Constitutional Authority

This document inherits from the [Product Constitution](../blueprint/00-product-constitution.md). Research informs product decisions but does not supersede the Constitution.

## Purpose

Collect evidence about veterinary practice operations, market structure, software pain, clinical workflows, staffing pressure, and adoption constraints.

## Goals

- Build a shared understanding of the veterinary software market.
- Ground product decisions in real clinic behavior.
- Identify operational patterns across independent, group, specialty, emergency, and corporate practices.
- Track constraints that affect sales, onboarding, migration, and support.

## Guiding Questions

- How do veterinary clinics actually spend time during a shift?
- Where do current systems slow teams down?
- Which workflows vary by clinic type, and which are nearly universal?
- What data, compliance, and communication constraints shape the market?
