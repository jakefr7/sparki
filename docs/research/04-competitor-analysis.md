# 04 Competitor Analysis

## Constitutional Authority

This document inherits from the [Product Constitution](../blueprint/00-product-constitution.md). Competitor analysis may inform tradeoffs, but it must not redefine Sparki's product laws.

## Purpose

Track the competitive landscape without letting competitor feature matrices define Sparki's product strategy.

## Goals

- Understand legacy systems and modern competitors.
- Identify workflow, migration, performance, communication, and AI gaps.
- Capture customer switching objections.
- Distinguish table-stakes capabilities from strategic differentiators.

## Guiding Questions

- What do clinics tolerate because switching is painful?
- Which competitor strengths are real and should be respected?
- Where do competitors optimize for modules instead of workflow?
- How do competitors handle migration, integrations, communication, and AI?
