# 05 User Personas

## Constitutional Authority

This document inherits from the [Product Constitution](../blueprint/00-product-constitution.md). Personas should clarify how constitutional principles meet real user pressure.

## Purpose

Define the people Sparki serves inside and around veterinary clinics, based on evidence from research and customer discovery.

## Goals

- Capture goals, pressures, workflows, and decision rights for each persona.
- Prevent generic persona writing disconnected from real clinic operations.
- Help product teams design for actual shift conditions.

## Guiding Questions

- Who uses Sparki during a live clinical workflow?
- Who decides whether Sparki is purchased?
- Who owns migration success?
- Who feels the pain when communication, records, billing, or handoffs fail?
- What does each persona need less of?

## Known Decisions

- Emotional load, context switching, and time pressure are core persona dimensions.
