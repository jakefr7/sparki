# 06 Clinic Workflows

## Constitutional Authority

This document inherits from the [Product Constitution](../blueprint/00-product-constitution.md). Workflow research should test and sharpen constitutional principles, not replace them.

## Purpose

Map the real-world workflows Sparki must support, simplify, and eventually improve.

## Goals

- Document clinical and operational workflows from patient intake through follow-up.
- Identify moments where current tools fragment patient context.
- Create a shared workflow vocabulary for product, design, engineering, and support.

## Guiding Questions

- What happens before, during, and after an appointment?
- Which handoffs cause information loss?
- Where does communication leave the patient record?
- Which workflows are high-frequency, high-risk, or high-friction?

## Known Decisions

- Workflows should be documented end to end, not as isolated software modules.
- Communication must be represented as part of patient workflow.

## Open Questions

- Which workflows define the first operating version of Sparki?
- Which workflow steps require auditability?
- Where can AI safely remove manual work?
