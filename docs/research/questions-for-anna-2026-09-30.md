# Questions for Anna

These questions help finish Sparki's medication, document, inventory, and referral workflows. Screenshots already supplied do not need to be sent again.

1. **Medication labels:** What printer make/model do you use, and what are the label's width and height? A photo of a blank label or the package is enough.
2. **Prescriptions:** Which fields must appear on your printed prescription? Do you sign on paper? Do you need outside-pharmacy prescriptions, in-clinic dispensing, or both? How do you handle controlled medications?
3. **Phone refills:** What does staff do from the initial phone request through veterinarian approval, pickup, payment, and any declined request? Sparki will support a medication-only encounter without an exam appointment.
4. **Inventory:** Which tasks cost the most time: receiving stock, finding a medication, low-stock review, expired lots, counts, transfers, or corrections? Do you track tablets/mL individually or whole packages?
5. **Eye exams:** Which measurements and findings need separate right-eye/left-eye fields? Which changes do you compare across visits? Is a general narrative ever insufficient?
6. **FAS:** Do you record a fear/anxiety/stress score today? If so, what scale do you use, who records it, and when is it updated? An example form is helpful.
7. **Pricing:** Do you enter the final selling price directly, or calculate it from cost plus markup? If calculated, what formula, rounding, and dispensing/minimum fees do you use?
8. **Referral handoff:** What documents go to the referring veterinarian, how are recipients chosen, and how are they sent? Would a clinic-maintained contact list work initially?
9. **Finance:** Which estimates, bundles, discounts, taxes, deposits, credits, and refunds are essential in your everyday work?
10. **Migration:** Can Pack Leader export patient/client information, medication history, medical records/PDFs, referral contacts, and outstanding balances? A de-identified sample export would help validate continuity.
