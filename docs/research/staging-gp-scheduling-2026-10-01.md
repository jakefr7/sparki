# Staging GP scheduling setup and hours research — 2026-10-01

## Purpose and scope

User requested typical GP appointment setup in Willow Creek staging and asked to look at “top 50 animal practice hours.” No ranking criterion was supplied. This is a convenience sample of 50 US GP locations with published hours, not a top-50 quality ranking or representative national survey. Reviewed official clinic pages on October 1, 2026; published hours may change.

The sample is heavily weighted to two groups (28 Modern Animal and 14 Small Door locations), so locations are not independent operating-model observations. Coming-soon Arcadia and Burlington and future Huntington Beach were excluded. Hours are local to each source clinic. Virtual/ER availability is not counted as GP office hours. Unspecified weekend hours remain unknown.

## Findings and staging choice

45 of 50 locations publish an 8 AM–6 PM weekday schedule (one also publishes those hours Saturday). This result is strongly affected by the chain weighting; do not call it a national norm or a best-practice standard. Saturday schedules vary or are unspecified. Office hours do not establish a particular provider’s bookable hours, lunch, procedure capacity, or staffing.

Saved staging weekly availability in America/Chicago: Monday–Friday 8 AM–6 PM, Saturday 9 AM–1 PM; no Sunday block. Saturday is a conservative operational choice, not the sample average. Blocks permit unassigned appointment types and any eligible provider; appointment-type eligibility limits exam visits to Anna Carter and the technician visit to Olivia Reed. These are fictional staging availability assumptions, not an actual clinic staffing commitment. No staff access changes.

## Verification and observations

All 13 appointment types and six weekly blocks were verified in the saved UI. Native time controls were needed: browser locator fill displayed edited time values but the first save retained 9–10 AM. Native accessibility setValue corrected Monday to 8 AM–6 PM and was used for remaining days. This is an automation-path limitation, not yet a demonstrated ordinary-user bug.

The cards continue to say “Weekly availability required” even after generic weekly blocks are saved; validate booking itself before treating the label as an actual setup failure. Miles Bennett is disabled; no re-enabling attempted.

Reload verification passed: 13 active appointment types and six active weekly blocks persisted. All types are listed in New Appointment; Wellness exposes Anna and Technician Visit exposes Olivia.

**Confirmed booking blocker:** Selecting Wellness Exam or Technician Visit from Finley's profile produces “We couldn’t load the Scheduler” and no available times. Try again did not resolve it. Reproduced from Scheduler → Day → October 2 → 8 AM → Wellness Exam, including explicitly selecting Anna. Future weekdays and Saturday are shown as “no times” while the request has failed; they are not proven to be fully booked or unavailable. Main Scheduler loads its empty calendar, so the booking failure is distinct from the whole page being unavailable. No appointment was created. Root cause and HTTP status are unverified; a captured React hydration error does not establish the cause.

UI follow-ups: distinguish failed availability loading from genuine “no times”; preserve booking drafts; show the actual patient photo; format owner phone consistently; replace remaining “Pet not found?” copy with Patient; clarify “Weekly availability required” on type cards when matching generic blocks already exist.
