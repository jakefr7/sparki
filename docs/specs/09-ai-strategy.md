# 09 AI Strategy

## Constitutional Authority

This document inherits from the [Product Constitution](../blueprint/00-product-constitution.md). AI strategy must follow the Constitution before any model, tool, or automation preference.

## Purpose

Define how Sparki uses AI to remove work from veterinary teams while preserving trust, safety, and workflow speed.

## Goals

- Identify AI use cases that reduce manual burden.
- Establish guardrails for clinical, operational, and communication assistance.
- Keep AI embedded in patient workflow.
- Avoid features that create extra review work or user distrust.

## Guiding Questions

- What work can AI safely prepare, summarize, classify, or automate?
- Where must a human remain clearly in control?
- What evidence does the user need to trust an AI suggestion?
- How should AI fail gracefully?

## Known Decisions

- AI should remove work rather than create more work.
- AI should operate inside the patient workflow.
- AI behavior must be evaluated for usefulness, safety, and trust.

## Open Questions

- Which AI use cases are appropriate for the first release?
- What data can AI access under early compliance assumptions?
- How will Sparki measure whether AI actually saves time?

## Notes

Do not frame AI as a destination. It is a capability that should make existing work faster, clearer, or less repetitive.
